## Content rules (extend the TASK-045 rules)
Same columns, variety (Latin American neutral), IPA, topics (8, each ≥ 8 % per level), example length (4–10 tokens at A2–B1, **4–14** at B2–C2: set `maxTokens` per level), notes (true, specific, ≤ 2 sentences, real Italian/Spanish contrasts and false friends).
- **Level fit:** words are chosen by frequency and usefulness for that CEFR level and never repeat an earlier level. Verbs in the infinitive, adjectives masculine singular, nouns singular with `el`/`la`.
- **Parts of speech per level** (±5 pp): nouns 40–45 %, verbs 25–30 %, adjectives 12–15 %, rest function words/adverbs; at B2–C2 more connectors (*sin embargo, aunque, mientras que*) and fixed phrases are allowed as `word` (multi-word allowed, ≤ 3 tokens).
- **Grammar tags** (every entry exactly one):
  - A2: `VERB_PRESENT_IRREGULAR, REFLEXIVE_VERBS, POR_PARA, PRETERITO_PERFECTO, PRETERITO_INDEFINIDO, DIRECT_OBJECT_PRONOUN` plus the A1 codes for nouns/adjectives/regular verbs.
  - B1: `PRETERITO_IMPERFECTO, FUTURO_SIMPLE, CONDICIONAL, IMPERATIVO, INDIRECT_OBJECT_PRONOUN, GUSTAR_VERBS`.
  - B2: `SUBJUNTIVO_PRESENTE, SUBJUNTIVO_IMPERFECTO, PERIFRASIS_VERBAL, PASIVA_REFLEJA, RELATIVOS`.
  - C1: `SUBJUNTIVO_COMPUESTO, ORACIONES_CONDICIONALES, SER_ESTAR_ADJETIVOS`.
  - C2: `CONCORDANCIA_TEMPORAL, REGISTRO_FORMAL`.
  A tag is used only when the example sentence **really exercises** it (a conjugated form of that tense/mood, a reflexive pronoun, an indirect pronoun, …); otherwise the entry gets the plain code of its part of speech. A new per-level check (`levelRules.<L>.allowedCodes`) rejects a code from a later level.
- **Choice-tagged sentences:** `SER_ESTAR` ≤ 20 per level at A2–B1 (≤ 10 at B2+ where it is `SER_ESTAR_ADJETIVOS` territory and not drilled); `POR_PARA`: **≤ 20 at A2, ≤ 20 at B1, ≤ 10 per level after** — only sentences where one of *por/para* is clearly right (purpose/destination/deadline → *para*; cause/means/duration/exchange → *por*) and the other would be wrong; exactly one of the two words in the sentence (the validator checks: contains exactly one of `por`/`para`, as a whole word, and not inside a fixed phrase list `por favor, por eso, por qué, para qué`); `DIRECT_OBJECT_PRONOUN`: ≤ 15 at A2 and B1, none later; only sentences where `lo/la/los/las` is a direct-object pronoun (not an article before a noun). The validator checks only that one of the four forms is present; the meaning is a human review item (see Definition of Done).
- **No sentence may be drilled in two choice rules** (one tag per entry anyway).
- **No offensive content; no invented words; neutral variety** (the forbidden list grows by what the review finds).
- **Generation:** ~35 rows per batch, topic mix per batch, validator after each level, a Spanish reader skims the sample files before 046b starts.
