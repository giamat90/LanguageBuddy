## Context

ChineseBuddy ships a pilot of 50 words (TASK-073). TASK-070 §6–7 sets the A1 target: about 300 words with their characters, machine-drafted,
checked by the validator and by native-speaker agents, then by a human native reader before any Play release.

---

## Scope

### In scope
- `scripts/wordbank/zh/A1.tsv`: 300 words. The 50 pilot words stay, spelled the same (saved words are keyed by the word).
- Coverage reference: the word list of HSK level 1 (2025 syllabus), as published by a third party. Only the choice of words follows it; meanings, examples,
  translations and notes are our own. No app text claims any link with HSK or its owners. Checking against the official syllabus is an owner step.
- `characters.tsv`: one row for every character used, and no other.
- `language.json`: new measure words; `segmented.allow` holds exactly the non-headword words the sentences use.
- `zh.ts`: a pinyin reading for every listed measure word.
- `scripts/check-unihan.mjs` + `scripts/lib/unihan.js`: compare the readings of `characters.tsv` with Unicode's `Unihan_Readings.txt`, which the owner downloads
  by hand into the gitignored `scripts/reference-lists/`. Reads only; changes nothing. `npm run check:unihan`.
- `WORDBANK_VERSION`, expectations table, CONTEXT.md.

### Out of scope
- Character layer (TASK-075), icon and store (TASK-078), A2, other UI languages, CC-CEDICT or any other dictionary text.

### Implementation notes
- Unihan lines are `U+4E00<TAB>kMandarin<TAB>yī`. Readings come from `kMandarin`, `kHanyuPinyin` (`10001.010:yī,yí`), `kHanyuPinlu` (`yī(32747)`),
  `kXHC1983` and `kTGHZ2013` (`1351.020:yī`). A row's reading is fine when Unihan has it; a reading without a tone mark is fine when Unihan has the syllable
  in any tone.
- Rules for content: 4–10 words per sentence ending 。？！, the headword exactly once; pinyin by word with 一 and 不 as pronounced; a choice tag only where
  one member of the set is possible; `measure_also` holds every other measure word that is also correct.
